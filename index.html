A five-minute survival roguelite

NAVOSNINJA

One ninja in khaki scrubs. A swarm of very silly monsters. Five minutes to survive.

  • Survive 5:00. A miniboss arrives at 2:30 and the final boss at 4:30.
  • Your sword and weapons attack automatically. Your job is to move, dodge and grab XP gems.
  • Level up to pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Max a weapon (Lv 5) to unlock its evolution.
